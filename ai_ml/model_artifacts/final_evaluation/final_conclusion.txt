Does CI-MICOP prove that AI is better than non-AI? Not universally, based on the current synthetic dataset. The experiments did not establish statistically meaningful predictive superiority: fraud screening PR-AUC differences between the rule baseline and ML models were within run-to-run variability under repeated grouped cross-validation, Risk_Category/Consistency/Excessive-Claim-Amount prediction were approximately chance level, multi-signal risk fusion did not significantly beat the single strongest signal, and Ant Colony Optimization did not consistently outperform a simple greedy surveyor-assignment baseline (10 of 19 directly-compared quantitative metrics nominally favored the AI/optimization method; nominal favorability was not confirmed as statistically significant in any of them). However, CI-MICOP demonstrates a broader decision-support capability through automated signal generation, multi-signal fusion, uncertainty estimation, explainability, and structured human-review prioritization - none of which the non-AI baseline provides in a comparably systematic, auditable form.
